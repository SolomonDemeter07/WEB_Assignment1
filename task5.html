Midterm Project: WH40K Fandom Wiki

Project Description

My midterm project is a comprehensive wiki-style website dedicated to the Warhammer 40,000 universe. The target users are both newcomers looking to understand the complex lore and veteran fans seeking a structured database of factions and history. The main purpose of the website is to provide an accessible, visually immersive, and well-organized hub for exploring the grimdark future of the 41st Millennium.

Sitemap (Navigation Structure)

The website will consist of the following 5 pages, all sharing a consistent gothic header, sidebar navigation, and footer:

  • 1. Home: Introduction to the WH40K universe and latest news.
  • 2. The Imperium of Man: Lore about the Emperor, Terra, and the Inquisition.
  • 3. Space Marines: A database of the Adeptus Astartes chapters and primarchs.
  • 4. Xenos & Chaos: Information regarding hostile factions (Orks, Tyranids, Chaos Gods).
  • 5. Join the Ranks: A contact and registration form for users to contribute to the wiki.
